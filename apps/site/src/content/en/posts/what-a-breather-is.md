A breather is an oscillation that stays localized in space while repeating in
time. Picture a long chain of coupled pendulums: most of the chain is nearly
still, but a few neighboring pendulums continue to exchange energy.

For site index $n$, a simple statement of localization is
$$\lim_{|n|\to\infty} u_n(t)=0.$$

The interesting part is not merely that the motion starts in one place. In a
nonlinear lattice, frequency depends on amplitude. Under the right conditions,
that moves the oscillation away from the spectrum of small linear waves, so
energy does not immediately disperse through the chain.

This draft will connect that idea to the Landau–Lifshitz equation after the
research wording has been reviewed.
